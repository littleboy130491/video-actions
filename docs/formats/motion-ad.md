# Format: Motion ad / promo

**Goal:** sell Imajiner (or one service) in 28–33 s + outro: stop the scroll, state the problem, show the work, list
the benefits, prove credibility, end on the brand outro. English copy unless told otherwise. Done once: the
Imajiner brand promo (engine `engines/imj`, scenes h5/p5/s5/e5 → approved final; r7 explored review fixes).

## Inputs
- Copy from imajiner.id (home "Imajiner helps you" points, about-page stats). Paraphrase allowed if the meaning stays.
- Brand: navy #024155, teal #2BB5BC, Poppins (Light/Regular/Medium), logo, white/off-white backgrounds.
- Portfolio: card screenshots from imajiner.id/portfolio (900×1152 portraits) + hero images; for featured client
  sites, the live site (fonts, hero video, layered images).
- Proof: Since 2019, 800+ clients (BUMN · Tbk · Corporates), 4.9 Google rating from 250+ reviews — keep current with
  the site; any score shown must be measured (e.g. Lighthouse).

## Structure that was approved
| Act | ≈ s | Content and motion |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 3.5–6 | "Building a website today? **Easy.**" / "Building one that actually works for your business? **NOT EASY.**" — frame-wide type; "Easy." one effortless move (teal); "NOT EASY." letters built with weight; sites play INSIDE the letters, camera zooms through the "." into the portfolio. An image in the first 1.5 s helps thumb-stop (r7). |
| Portfolio | 5–7 | Masonry of real sites (portraits + landscapes), parallax columns, staggered mask/scale reveals, slow diagonal camera travel; folds/zooms away into "That's where we come in." on a plain background. No flickery 0.3 s cuts, no cheap carousel. |
| Benefits | 12–14 | "Imajiner helps you" then 4 points, each ≈ 2.5–3 s with its own meaningful visual and a camera transition into the next: 01 Bring ideas to life = hand-drawn pen sketch on paper (wobbly variable-width strokes) turning into a real site; 02 Designs that stand out = grey placeholder grid, one real client site (with its hero video) rises, shown desktop-size; 03 Fast, secure, easy to update = proof on one big site (load bar, padlock + https, live inline headline edit + "Published"); 04 Fast and helpful support = big WhatsApp-style chat (typing…, blue ticks). |
| Proof | 4–6 | Stats as PUNCHES, one per full frame: "Since 2019" → "800+ clients / BUMN · Tbk · Corporates" → "4.9 ★★★★★ Google rating / Based on 250+ reviews"; strong scale-in, hold ≥ 1.2 s, punchy camera move to the next; navy/white alternation; to white. |
| Outro | 6.5 | Standard outro (prebuilt). No CTA / end card before it (owner decision). |

## Art direction (owner decisions — keep unless told otherwise)
- "Minimalist luxury": plain white / warm off-white / navy or soft gradients; restraint; hairlines; refined type.
- **Never text over images.** Images beside/below text, or inside letters.
- Luxury ≠ static: strong but silky camera work (push-ins, pull-backs, zoom-throughs, match moves), no shakes.
- Big: headlines ≥ 128 px, visuals 70–100 % of the frame width (desktop sites may bleed off one edge).
